## Best Practices

1. **Start with GetAnswers and GetMailer** - Most AI-focused, best fit for AI directories
2. **Highlight free tiers** - Many directories prioritize free tools
3. **Use consistent branding** - Same logo, colors, messaging
4. **Track with UTM parameters** - Add `?utm_source=directoryname` to URLs
5. **Submit one product per day** - Avoid looking like spam
6. **Check approval status** - Many directories take 1-2 weeks
